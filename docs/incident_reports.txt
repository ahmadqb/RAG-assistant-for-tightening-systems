INCIDENT REPORT #2024-087
Date: 2024-09-15
Machine: MCH-03 (angle head 3W090)
Issue: Repeated E-451 errors causing production downtime
Root cause: Ambient temperature in the tightening cell reached 38°C due to
failed HVAC unit. The measurement transducer drifted and triggered calibration errors.
Resolution: Maintenance replaced HVAC filter and recalibrated the transducer.
Production resumed after 2 hours of downtime.
Preventive action: Installed temperature monitoring alert at 33°C threshold.

INCIDENT REPORT #2024-102
Date: 2024-10-03
Machine: MCH-01
Issue: Operator injury - minor hand laceration
Root cause: Operator removed safety guard without following LOTO procedure
while clearing a jam at the tightening spindle.
Resolution: Operator received first aid, no lost time. Retraining scheduled
for all tightening cell operators on LOTO procedures.
Preventive action: Installed interlock switch on safety guard to prevent
operation when guard is open.

INCIDENT REPORT #2024-118
Date: 2024-10-20
Machine: MCH-01 (angle head 3W050, max. torque 50 Nm)
Issue: E-460 torque above rated maximum, measured 52.4 Nm, cycle aborted
Root cause: After a program update, the target torque of one tightening job was set
too high, so the measured torque exceeded the 50 Nm rating of the 3W050 angle head.
Resolution: Tightening program corrected, angle head inspected, no damage found.
Production resumed after 45 minutes of downtime.
Preventive action: Program releases must be checked against the rated maximum torque
of the installed angle head before going live.
